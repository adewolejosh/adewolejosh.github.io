It, indeed, was a quiet year for me, pretty falling under the radar of "I lost my spark" decade. On coding, I would say I contributed a whole lot at work. Asides, it is a beautiful, well-thought-out and designed language and with so much potential. To practice more, I spent a lot more of my Leetcoding with using Rust and it has indeed in some ways improved my Rust (though I must confess, I don't still feel confident in some part of the language - pointing to the fact, I may need to work on projects outside the domain I used it at work for). Interestingly started the year similarly with cpp but never went too deep, thankfully carried the learnings there into my Rust learning and have one project at work, zero personal "FOR NOW". Spent more time with Python and TypeScript, and Dart. Looking to do more Math and Engineering.
